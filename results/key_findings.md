# Key Findings — AdventureWorks Sales Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
This report summarizes findings from a sales analysis of the AdventureWorks database using Microsoft SQL Server and T-SQL.

## 2. Annual Revenue Performance
- **2013 revenue:** 43,622,479.05
- **2014 revenue:** 20,057,928.81
- **Year-over-year change:** approximately -54.02%

**Interpretation:** Revenue declined substantially between 2013 and 2014. Further investigation would be needed to determine the underlying business causes.

## 3. Product Performance
- **Highest-revenue product across the analyzed dataset:** Mountain-200 Black, 38.
- **Highest-quantity product:** AWC Logo Cap.
- Several products experienced substantial revenue declines between 2013 and 2014.

**Interpretation:** Product performance varied, and the largest percentage declines did not necessarily represent the largest monetary losses.

## 4. Territory Performance
Revenue declined between 2013 and 2014 across all analyzed territories.

**Interpretation:** The decline was widespread rather than isolated to a single territory. The magnitude of the decline differed by territory.

## 5. Order Performance
- Orders declined from 14,182 in 2013 to 11,761 in 2014.
- Average revenue per order declined from approximately 3,075.90 to 1,705.46.

**Interpretation:** The revenue decline coincided with fewer orders and lower average revenue per order.

## 6. Recommendations
- Investigate the drivers of the revenue decline.
- Review products with large monetary or percentage declines.
- Compare territory-level trends to identify areas requiring further investigation.
- Monitor order volume and average revenue per order.

## 7. Limitations
- The analysis identifies patterns but does not establish the causes of the revenue decline.
- A product with no recorded revenue in a year is not necessarily discontinued.
- Findings are limited to the data and period analyzed.
